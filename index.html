Besan Laado

Our namesake. Roasted gram flour and ghee.

One family recipe, now a whole kitchen.

LAADO began with a grandmother's besan laado. People kept asking for more, so we added Plain Rice-dal-Vegetables, paneer,fresh rotis,parathas,veg-biryani and snacks with sweets.

We use seasonal vegetables, whole spices and pure ghee. No artificial colours, no shortcuts. We also cater for parties, pujas and office lunches.
